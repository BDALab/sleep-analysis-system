\section*{Supplementary classification methods}

The secondary HC-versus-preDLB analysis used nested five-fold stratified
person-grouped cross-validation. All visits belonging to one underlying person
remained in the same fold. Preprocessing, median imputation, residualisation,
feature selection, XGBoost hyperparameter search, and threshold selection were
performed within the training data of each outer fold.

The randomised search optimised inner-fold balanced accuracy and evaluated 12
configurations per outer fold for recursive feature elimination (RFE) and 20
per fold for each univariate $K$-best pipeline. Candidate XGBoost values were:
150, 300, or 500 trees; learning rate 0.01, 0.03, 0.05, 0.1, or 0.2; gamma 0,
0.05, 0.1, 0.25, or 0.5; maximum depth 2--6; subsample 0.6, 0.8, or 1.0;
column subsampling by level 0.6, 0.8, or 1.0; column subsampling by tree 0.5,
0.7, 0.9, or 1.0; minimum child weight 1, 3, 5, or 10; $L_1$ regularisation
0, 0.01, 0.1, or 1; $L_2$ regularisation 0.5, 1, 2, or 5; and positive-class
weight 1--5. No synthetic over-sampling or under-sampling was used. RFE
considered 20, 40, 80, or 120 selected features and step sizes 0.05, 0.1, or
0.2. $K$-best considered 20, 40, 80, 120, or all available features.

The default threshold was 0.5. Each tuned threshold maximised Matthews
correlation coefficient over probabilities generated by grouped inner-fold
out-of-fold prediction in the corresponding outer training partition.
Confidence intervals used 10,000 diagnosis-stratified person-level bootstrap
samples (seed 1701, incremented deterministically by analysis), resampling
people with replacement and retaining all visits belonging to each sampled
person. This preserves the repeat-visit clustering in the uncertainty estimate.

\section*{Machine-readable supporting files}

The accompanying files \texttt{classification\_uncertainty.csv},
\texttt{classification\_calibration.csv},
\texttt{classification\_missingness\_and\_flow.csv}, and
\texttt{classification\_fold\_hyperparameters.csv}, together with
\texttt{classification\_search\_space.json}, contain the unrounded
estimates, bootstrap settings, analytical flow, and foldwise selected
hyperparameters. They were generated from saved out-of-fold predictions by
\path{analysis/tripod_classification_audit.py}. The final archival commit
and persistent release identifier will be added only after collaborator review.
